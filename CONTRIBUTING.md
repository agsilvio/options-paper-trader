# Contributing

Changes are welcome. Open an issue first for anything large, then send a pull request.

By submitting a contribution you confirm that you wrote it (or have the right to submit it) and that it may be distributed under this project's license, GNU GPL v3.0 or later. The maintainer may also relicense the project in the future, and your submission is made on that basis.

If you publish a modified copy of the extension, the GPL requires you to make its full source available under the same license. Sending your improvements back as a pull request is the easiest way to meet that and keeps one shared version.
